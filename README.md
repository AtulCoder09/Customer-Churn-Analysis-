# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end data analysis project exploring telecommunications customer churn behavior to identify key churn drivers, evaluate customer retention risks, and deliver actionable business insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🛠️ Project Workflow
1. **Data Source:** Extracted the raw dataset (`WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn`) from [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/).
2. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** 
   * Performed initial inspection, formatting, and outlier handling in **Microsoft Excel**.
   * Conducted further data transformation, handled missing values, and structured data types using a **Jupyter Notebook (Python)**.
3. **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Designed an interactive, multi-page business intelligence dashboard using **Power BI**.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Overview & Key Metrics

* **Total Customers:** 7,043
* **Active Customers:** 5,174 (73.46%)
* **Churned Customers:** 1,869 (26.54% Overall Churn Rate)

### Key Insights Discovered:
* **Contract Type:** Month-to-month contracts exhibit the highest volume and proportion of customer churn compared to locked-in one-year and two-year plans.
* **Payment Methods:** Customers using **Electronic Check** as a payment method show significantly higher churn rates compared to those on automated bank transfers or credit cards.
* **Internet Services:** Fiber optic customers account for a notable share of churned users compared to DSL or non-users.
* **Tenure Impact:** Newer customers with low tenure and specific monthly charge brackets show peak vulnerability to churning.
